# Changelog

All notable changes to the **BRAUN RB-26** Master Studio Reverberator & Space Synthesizer are documented in this file.

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.5.1] - 2026-11-19

### Fixed
- **Windows Standalone Dual-Clock Drift Popping with VB-Cable & Virtual/Hardware Endpoint Pairs (`StandaloneAudioGuard.h`, `Rb26StandaloneApp.cpp`)**:
  - Replaced stock JUCE single-thread full-duplex WASAPI/DirectSound coupling in `BRAUN_RB26_Standalone` (`JUCE_USE_CUSTOM_PLUGIN_STANDALONE_APP=1`) with `DriftCompensatedAudioIODeviceType` and `DuplexDriftCompensatedAudioIODevice`, isolating capture (e.g. `CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)` QPC clock) and render (hardware crystal oscillator) onto independent single-direction OS audio threads bridged by `ElasticDriftRingBuffer`.
  - Eliminated both permanent capture-endpoint overflow packet drops (`IAudioCaptureClient::ReleaseBuffer(0)`) under positive clock drift (`f_in > f_out`) and repeated `zeromem` zero-splice clicks under negative clock drift (`f_in < f_out`) — including in Standby passthrough (`!isPoweredOn`) and 0% wet mix — via a pre-primed 64-bit lock-free SPSC circular buffer, 4-point 3rd-order Hermite fractional interpolation, and an overdamped gain-scheduled 2nd-order PI drift controller ($\zeta = 1.15, \omega_n = 0.025\text{ rad/s}$).
  - Added click-free 128-sample raised-cosine dual-head recentering on severe output stalls and smooth exponential decay/fade-in recovery on capture underruns, plus automatic asynchronous sample-rate conversion when Exclusive/Low-Latency endpoints are locked to distinct native rates (e.g. $44.1\text{ kHz}$ vs $48.0\text{ kHz}$).
- **Laptop Sleep / S0 Modern Standby Wake Recovery & Invalidated WASAPI Storm Guard (`StandaloneAudioGuard.h`)**:
  - Added `WindowsPowerSuspendNotifier` (hidden top-level `WS_POPUP` `WM_POWERBROADCAST` listener + `PowerRegisterSuspendResumeNotification` for Windows 10/11 S0 Low Power Idle / Modern Standby lid close/reopen) and `StandaloneAudioRecoveryController`.
  - Cleanly releases audio endpoints on `PBT_APMSUSPEND` and restores the preserved `AudioDeviceSetup` across progressive backoff retries on `PBT_APMRESUMEAUTOMATIC` / `PBT_APMRESUMESUSPEND` even when `AudioDeviceManager::setAudioDeviceSetup` transiently fails and clears its internal `currentSetup` while Realtek/USB/HDMI codecs transition from `D3` to `D0`.
  - Added a physically invariant callback-rate storm guard (`>= 24` blocks and `>= 200 ms` of audio frames elapsing in `< 25%` of real-time wall-clock duration) to detect and heal post-sleep `AUDCLNT_E_DEVICE_INVALIDATED` unthrottled spin loops, plus non-disruptive hot capture reopen when input-only WASAPI `WaitForSingleObject(1000 ms)` times out.
- **Windows Background EcoQoS / Timer Power-Throttling Opt-Out, Occluded WebView2 IPC Suspension & Idle Reset Optimization (`StandaloneAudioGuard.h`, `PluginEditor.cpp`, `Rb26Engine.cpp`)**:
  - Added `WindowsAudioProcessGuard` in `BRAUN_RB26.exe` to opt out of `ProcessPowerThrottling` (`PROCESS_POWER_THROTTLING_EXECUTION_SPEED` and `PROCESS_POWER_THROTTLING_IGNORE_TIMER_RESOLUTION`, with automatic fallback for Windows 10 1709–1909 builds) and activate `1 ms` multimedia timer resolution (`timeBeginPeriod(1)`), preventing CPU parking and `15.625 ms` timer coalescing when RB-26 runs unattended in the background.
  - Added Win32 child + `GA_ROOT` ancestor visibility/iconic checks (`isWebViewActuallyShowing()`) and per-host `UserDataFolder` scoping in `BRAUN_RB26AudioProcessorEditor`, halting 120 Hz JSON telemetry/scope IPC while minimized or hidden and re-synchronizing parameters cleanly on restore.
  - Added `mPitchBuffersDirty` tracking in `Rb26ReverbEngine` to skip redundant `5.5 MiB` pitch-shifter and pitch-delay `std::fill` wipes on real-time idle silence transitions when shimmer/dimmer sends are inactive.

## [1.5.0] - 2026-11-14

### Fixed
- **Shepard-Risset Barber-Pole Shimmer Direction & Continuity (`ShepardPitchSpiral`, `PitchShifter`)**:
  - Replaced unsigned `std::abs(1.0f - rm) / mWindowSamples` with the exact signed delay-slope phase velocity `(1.0f - rm) / mWindowSamples` and float32-safe `[0, 1)` phase wrap on both stereo channels (`mVoicesL` and `mVoicesR`). Upward `BarberShimmer` voices (`rm > 1`) now produce genuine upward pitch shifts ($f_{\text{out}} = r_m f_{\text{in}}$) with $C^1$ continuity across unison (`rm = 1`) instead of inverting into downward shifts.
  - Calibrated `PitchShifter`'s internal `mShepardSpiral` depth to `1.0f` in `prepare()` and `setSpiralParameters()` so `PitchShifter::processSample`'s `(1 - spiralDepth) * raw + spiralDepth * spiralOut` blend obeys a linear crossfade law without `depth^2` double-attenuation or dry bleed, and persisted `ShepardPitchSpiral::mDepth` across `prepare()`.
- **192 kHz Delay Buffer Truncation & Premature Idle Silence Gate (`Rb26Engine`, `PitchShifter`, `ShepardPitchSpiral`)**:
  - Expanded `Rb26ReverbEngine::kPreDelayBufferCapacity` and `kPitchDelayCapacity` from $131{,}072$ to $262{,}144$ ($2^{18}$), eliminating $192\text{ kHz}$ bitmask wrap-around truncation when `pitchDelayMs = 500 ms` with the $1.05 \times 1.375$ stereo offset ($723.75\text{ ms} = 138{,}960\text{ samples}$).
  - Expanded `DualTapDelayPitchShifter::kMaxCapacity` and `ShepardPitchSpiral::kMaxCapacity` from $65{,}536$ to $131{,}072$ ($2^{17}$) and `PitchShifter::kCircCapacity` from $32{,}768$ to $65{,}536$ ($2^{16}$), preventing truncation of the $211\text{ ms}$ Dimmer circulation line ($40{,}512\text{ samples}$ at $192\text{ kHz}$) and $200\text{ ms}$ `-24 st` grain windows.
  - Extended `Rb26ReverbEngine`'s idle silence gate threshold with a sample-rate-scaled $50\text{ ms}$ guard plus maximum active pre-delay (`current` and `target`) and `max(activePitchDelaySamples, activeErSamples)`, preventing premature silence clamping of discrete late Early Reflection taps (up to $172.4\text{ ms}$ at `roomSize = 4.0`) and long pitch-delayed echoes when `decayRt60Sec` is short.
- **Early Reflections Rapid Automation Continuity & FDN Multi-Channel Pitch Feedback (`EarlyReflections`, `FdnReverbTank`)**:
  - Scaled `EarlyReflections` dual-bank tap crossfade length proportionally with sample rate (`~6 ms`, clamped `[64, 2048]`) and queued incoming `setParameters()` tap updates via `mPendingTapUpdate` while an active crossfade is in flight, eliminating mid-crossfade tap jump clicks under rapid `roomSize` automation.
  - Fixed `FdnReverbTank::processSampleMultiChannel` so `fbR` reads `pitchFb[1]` independently of `numInChannels`, preserving stereo pitch-diffusion feedback on Mono-In -> Stereo/Surround-Out bus layouts, and clamped `effectiveOutChannels` to `<= 12`.
- **Thread-Safe Power/Reset Lifecycle, Standby MIDI Isolation & Auto-Wake (`PluginProcessor`, `AcousticExciter`, `PluginEditor`)**:
  - Made `BRAUN_RB26AudioProcessor::setPower(bool)` idempotent (`if (prev != powered)`) so redundant `setPower(true)` calls on editor open or state sync never wipe an active reverb tail.
  - Eliminated cross-thread data races in `PluginProcessor::reset()` and `AcousticExciterEngine` by deferring resets to `processBlock` and advancing consumer-owned `mFifoReadPos` (`discardPendingTriggers()`) without mutating producer-owned `mFifoWritePos`.
  - Preserved queued SPSC exciter triggers across Standby auto-wake (`mPendingExciterFifoFlush = !powered`), processed pending engine resets prior to block MIDI/FIFO execution, ignored MIDI Note-On events while in Standby (honoring Note-Off / CC 64), disarmed persisted `freeze_hold` on initial Standalone cold-boot `setStateInformation` (`wrapperType_Standalone`), and added `mStateSyncDirty` / `hasPendingActivity()` so host preset/state recalls and Standby Dirac/Pink/Mallet auto-wake triggers synchronize cleanly across Audio, Native UI, and WebView2 threads.
- **WebView2 IPC Echo Loop, Resource Sandbox, Parameter Scaling & 4-Manifold WebAudio Engine (`web/js/app.js`, `web/js/audio/rb26_web_engine.js`, `web/js/ui/vector-pad.js`, `PluginEditor.cpp`, `Rb26Engine.cpp`)**:
  - Added `fromHost` guards to `BraunRb26App.setPower(isPowered, fromHost = false)` and `togglePoisson(fromHost = false)` plus `isPoissonDisable` filtering in `PluginEditor::handleExciterTriggerFromWeb()` so incoming host `paramUpdate` Standby transitions never echo `power`, `stopRecording`, or `{ type: 'poisson', enable: false }` auto-wake events back to JUCE.
  - Hardened `PluginEditor::getResource()` against raw and URL-encoded (`%2e%2e`) path traversal and Windows drive-letter escapes (`:`), enforcing strict `juce::File::isAChildOf(baseDir)` confinement across all disk fallback lookups.
  - Corrected `tail_mod_depth_ms` scaling between Web UI (`0–100%`) and APVTS (`0.0–5.0 ms`, default `2.25 ms` = `45%`), unified `tail_mod_rate` `0.05–5.00 Hz` (`4.95 Hz` span) power-curve mapping across `BraunVectorPad` and `app.js` with bidirectional host `paramUpdate` -> `vectorPad.setCoordinates` sync and cold-boot `lastUserInteractionTime > 0` gating, aligned 6 rotary knob bounds (`predelay` max `500 ms`, `damping_low` max `4.00x`, `mono_bass` min `20 Hz`, `rt60_decay` min `0.2 s`, `tail_mod_rate` max `5.00 Hz`, `tail_bloom` max `300 ms`) with `Parameters.h`, and synchronized all 15 factory presets 1:1 across `Rb26Engine.cpp` (`presets.reserve(15)`), `web/js/app.js`, and `factory_presets.json` (`SUB_TRITONE_ABYSS` calibrated to `shimmer_interval: +7 st`, `dimmer_interval: -12 st`).
  - Implemented distinct 8-line FDN delay distributions and dual-bank S-curve crossfading for all 4 geometric manifolds (`Poincare`, `Whispering Gallery`, `Anharmonic Plate`, `Stockhausen Klangdom`) in `Rb26WebEngine`, persisted `manifold_type` in `getSnapshot()`, and unified `setPower(true)` input trim staging (`1.0x`).
- **Documentation, Windows PE Metadata, Packaging & GFM Math Compliance**:
  - Wired `OBJECT_DEPENDS` on `BRAUN_RB26_resources.rc` in `CMakeLists.txt` so Windows PE `VERSIONINFO` (`FileVersion` / `ProductVersion` = `1.5.0`) regenerates deterministically across version bumps, and enforced Win32 PE `VERSIONINFO` verification plus stale archive cleanup in `scripts/package_release.py`.
  - Registered all 10 C++ verification suites in CTest (`source/tests/CMakeLists.txt`), synchronized all documentation and `wiki/` guides to `v1.5.0` (29 APVTS parameters, 23 rotary knobs, 15 factory presets), and resolved all GFM/KaTeX lint findings across all 27 repository and wiki markdown files.
